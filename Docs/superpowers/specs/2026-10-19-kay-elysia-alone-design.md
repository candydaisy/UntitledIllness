# Kay and Elysia, alone — design

**Goal.** More time with Kay and Elysia by themselves: Kay teases her when nobody's around, she gets flustered (and once, actually hurt), with more happy slice-of-life moments and more moments where one of them lets the other see something.

**Routes.** Kay and Elysia. Each new scene is written twice, once from each side. Under canon, every shared line matches word for word (Canon rule 2). Neither route shows the other's thoughts (rule 1).

## Who they are together

- Teasing is Kay's real private self, not a mask. It's about her slime-nerd side, her going red ("the ears thing"), her bossiness ("yes, ma'am"), and callbacks (the pad kra pao line).
- Elysia pouts, hits his arm, says "K!!", and gets him back. Once, before the museum, the teasing lands on the old wound.
- As Kay's weight climbs, the teasing fades. Elysia notices that before anything else.
- Affection stays soft: hands, shoulders, a hoodie.
- In Elysia's route, choices alone with Kay are never timed. Nobody is waiting on her.
