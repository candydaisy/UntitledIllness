# Untitled Illness — Canon Timeline

This is what *actually happens* during the semester when every route is played with its **canon choice**: the choice that character would really make. In each route's story file, canon choices are marked **★**.

Every route covers the same ten weeks from a different person's eyes. A shared scene, like the presentation, the front step, or the gate on Monday, plays out word for word the same in every route when the canon choice is taken. Non-canon choices let the player change things, but only inside that one playthrough.

## Rules for writing other perspectives

1. **Nobody can read anyone else's mind.** A route only shows what its character sees, hears, and is told. Anything else is a guess, and guesses can be wrong. Wrong guesses are the point, because the player may already know the truth from another route.
2. **Shared dialogue is fixed under canon.** If Kay says "It's fine. It was a long time ago." in his route, he says it in Ulyn's route too.
3. **Each route's choices are about that character's own life:** their family, their friends, and what they do when nobody's watching.
4. **Every route uses Emotional Weight the same way.** Low weight means more room to breathe. Critical means fewer options, and *helpful* choices (reaching out) lock.

## Before the story

- **About five years ago:** Kay's dad leaves. Kay's mom cries at the kitchen table for weeks. Twelve-year-old Kay decides to be "the easy one."
- **Eleven years ago:** Elysia and Mint meet in kindergarten.
- **Elysia, third grade:** A boy smears slime from a rotten log inside her bag and the class laughs for a week. She decides she'll never be weird again.
- **First day of Grade 10:** Kay and Ulyn become friends. For most of that year, they tell each other everything.
- **Last February–March:** Ulyn's parents separate. His dad moves out. His mom cries in the bathroom with the shower running. Ulyn starts sleeping at Tan's.
- **Last March, 3 a.m.:** Kay messages Ulyn: *"can I call you."* Ulyn doesn't answer for four days, then sends *"sorry, was busy."* Kay never asks for anything again. Neither of them ever mentions it.
- **End of last semester:** Kay and Elysia start dating. At school, Kay keeps his distance.
- **This semester, before Week 1:** The agriculture club is gone, and its garden behind the science building has died. Kay's water heater breaks a week before Day 1.

## Week 1

### Monday (Day 1)

- **Kay** finishes Bank and Fern's slides at 2:30 a.m. He oversleeps, takes a cold shower, and arrives at the gate looking "like a zombie."
- **Elysia** was on the phone with Ploy until 3 a.m. (boyfriend trouble). At the gate she turns around like she felt Kay there and waves. Ploy pulls her away.
- **Ulyn** wakes up on Tan's couch with an unanswered text from his mom: *coming home tomorrow? making khao man gai.* He answers *can't tmr. council stuff.* At the gate he guesses right that Kay did the slides again.
- **Presentation:** Bank blanks. Kay covers immediately. The teacher praises the "teamwork." Ulyn says nothing, watching Kay.
- **After class:** Ulyn: *"You're always like this, Kay."* Kay: *"They just forgot. It's not a big deal."* Ulyn: *"Right. They forgot. For the third time."* Both wonder if the other is mad at them. Neither is.
- **Lunch:** Ulyn offers to skip his chemistry report. Kay tells him to go. In the library, Ulyn sees through the window that Kay is in the pad kra pao line with Elysia, and he's relieved.
- **Elysia** got in that line on purpose, because Kay always gets pad kra pao on Mondays. *"Because it's good?"* She rolls her eyes. She invites him to sit with her, and he does. She tells her friends to stop staring and bumps his foot under the table. Afterward, Ploy calls Kay "kind of weird." Elysia laughs it off: *"He's just shy."*
- **Home ec:** "The most common way people kill a plant is overwatering it." Kay thinks of his front yard. Ulyn thinks of Kay.
- **After school:** In front of her friends, Elysia asks Kay to walk home with her. Kay: *"I... shouldn't. You don't want people looking at us like that."* Her smile stays on a beat too long. She decides he's embarrassed to be seen with her. **(Wrong.)**
- **Elysia** tells Mint on the phone that it hurt. Mint offers to "fight him."
- **Ulyn** volunteers to run half of sports day.
- **Kay** lies on his bed wondering if Elysia is upset, types a message, and deletes it. He scrolls cat videos for two hours.
- **Night:** Ulyn texts Kay: *you good? you seemed off today... not kay-sure.* Kay: *yeah, i'm sure. don't worry.* Ulyn: *ok. i'm here if you wanna talk. like whenever.*
- **Kay** lies awake. *"Today was fine... Right?"*

### Tuesday

- Elysia drafts *why didn't you want to walk with me* and deletes it. She sends *goooood morning!! ☀️ did u eat breakfast* instead. Kay: *morning! not yet lol.* Neither mentions yesterday.
- **Community Project** announced. Group 3 stays together, with Ulyn joining. Ulyn suggests the dead garden. He doesn't say why: since his dad left, his mom's balcony plants have all died. Fern asks Kay to write the report. Kay: *"Sure. I'll handle the report."* Ulyn's pen stops. He says nothing.
- **Lunch:** Ulyn finished his report at 3 a.m. and lies with his face on the table.
- **Kay** tells Mom he's "making tea" with the kettle. He still hasn't told her the heater is broken.

### Thursday

- **7-Eleven, 8:15 p.m.** Mom's on a night shift. Elysia, out of tutoring on the next street, finds Kay buying one toastie for dinner. Away from school he teases her: three bags of shrimp crackers "for Pun," and the pad kra pao line, *"a coincidence every Monday for two months."* She hits him with her folder and puts a strawberry yogurt drink in his basket "as a punishment." He walks her the hundred meters to her bus stop. *"Eat your toastie." "Yes, ma'am."*
- **Elysia** doesn't ask why he'll walk with her here but not on Monday. On the bus she decides it's her friends he's hiding her from. **(Still wrong.)**

### Saturday

- First garden workday. Kay arrives at 7:40, Ulyn at 8:20 with bread, Fern at 9:00 for photos only. Bank doesn't come. Ms. Noi brings seeds: *"Only if the soil is dry."* Kay waters twice a day anyway.

## Week 2

- **Wednesday:** Elysia skips Ploy's mall trip, tells them she has to help her brother, and goes to the garden to see Kay. She finds a slime mold on a log and catches herself mid-sentence. Kay: *"I care. Keep going."* She talks for twenty minutes. Kay: *"Should I be jealous?"* Elysia: *"K!!"* *"It solved a maze. What have you ever solved?" "Bank's slides." "That's not a maze. That's a hostage situation."* At the gate: *"You look tired, K." "I'm always tired." "That's what worries me."*
- **Thursday:** Mint stops Kay outside the library: *"She's been tired lately... You two are the same kind of idiot."* **Elysia doesn't know this happened.**
- **Saturday night:** Video call, chemistry homework. She makes him fry eggs (*"Yes, ma'am."*). She admits she was up till three with Ploy. She falls asleep mid-question. He does her questions 6 and 7 and tells her she snores *"like a cat."* *"DELETE THIS CHAT."*

## Week 3

- Elysia's nightly calls with Ploy continue.
- In art class, Ploy mocks Nam's plush keychain. Elysia laughs along, and feels sick about it all afternoon.
- **Tuesday, after art class:** She goes to the garden. Kay tries to cheer her up: *"Careful. Next you'll be keeping slime in your bag."* He doesn't know about third grade. She says *"Haha. It's fine!"*, which is the version, done with Kay for the first time. He hears his own "it's fine" in it. *"...Sorry. That was a dumb joke." "It was a little dumb. It's not you. Long day."*
- **Friday:** Ulyn falls asleep in history. Kay notices the declined call from "Mom" and asks: *"You okay? You look wrecked."* Ulyn: *"...Yeah. Just sports day stuff. Thanks for asking, though."* It's the first time anyone has asked him in months. He declines the next call from his mom too.
- The first seedlings sprout. *proud dad.* Elysia, by text, in four seconds: *ANY SLIME???* It's her way of saying they're okay.

## Week 4

- **Pun** comes home and says the kids at school call him "slime boy." Elysia tells him, *"Maybe keep the slime at home."* She hates herself for it.
- **Friday, sports day:** Ulyn runs it, and everything works. His mom comes to watch. He says he's "too busy, later," and she leaves a container of khao man gai at the council tent with a note. He eats it alone behind the gym. Kay works the water station, looking grey. Elysia brings him a cold drink in front of her friends and runs off before anyone can tease her.
- **5 p.m., the bleachers:** Elysia tells Kay that Pun got called slime boy and that she told him to keep the slime at home. Kay: *"I do that too. It doesn't always work."* Elysia: *"You didn't make fun of me once today... That's how I know something's wrong."* Kay: *"It's just the sun."* She doesn't believe him. She holds his hand anyway, then asks him to tease her about something. *"Your voice sounds like a duck."*

## Week 5 (Midterms)

- **Tuesday:** Bank asks Kay to do his research section. Kay writes three pages between 11 p.m. and 1:40 a.m.
- **Wednesday, the library:** Elysia's flashcards. Kay falls asleep on her shoulder for forty minutes, and she lets Ploy's calls buzz. *"Also, you drool." "...Not like a cat?" "Cats are dignified. Now we're even."* *"You look tired." "I'm always tired." "...That's what worries me."* (Week 2's lines, the other way round.) *"K. Are you okay? Actually?" "It's fine. Just midterms."* She knows that answer. She uses it herself.
- Tan asks Ulyn when he's going home. *"After exams."*
- Ploy and her boyfriend break up, and Elysia is on the phone every night. Her mom says she's on her phone too much. Elysia just says *"sorry."*
- **Thursday:** Kay is top of the class again and feels nothing. The garden's chili is yellow and the basil is rotting. Ms. Noi: *"They're drowning."*
- **Thursday night:** Kay thinks *everyone would be fine without me.* He looks at Ulyn's name in his chats and puts the phone face down without texting.

## Week 6

### Thursday

- **Kay** can't get up. He sits on the edge of his bed for forty minutes, then lies back down. He tells Mom "bye" like everything's normal.
- **8:10.** Ulyn: *where are u. ur never late.*
- **8:15 / 8:40.** Elysia: *K? didn't see u at the gate 👀* and *are you sick??*
- **9:02.** Bank asks about the garden key. Kay answers: *spare key's under the blue pot.*
- **~9:40.** Kay to Elysia: *i couldn't get up today.* Elysia: *ok. thank u for telling me. do u want me to call or do u want me to leave u alone* · *leave me alone for now. sorry* · *don't be sorry. i'm here.*
- **Lunch:** Elysia finds Ulyn and asks him to go see Kay. She respects that Kay asked her to leave him alone, but she thinks Ulyn is "different." **Kay never learns that Elysia sent him.**
- **After school:** Ulyn tells the student council he has a stomachache and skips the sports day wrap-up meeting. He buys pink milk, crispy pork, and the coconut cookies Kay ate every day in Grade 10.
- **4:10, the front step.** Ulyn confesses about March. Kay says "It's fine." Ulyn: *"Don't do that. Not kay-sure. Please."* Kay: *"...I thought I was too much."* Kay tells him about his dad, which Ulyn never knew. Then he says *"some nights I think everyone would be fine without me."* Ulyn stays calm and thanks him. He asks Kay to talk to someone and names Ms. Noi. Then Ulyn admits he isn't okay either. Kay: *"You decline your mom's calls in class."* Ulyn didn't know anyone had noticed.
- **7 p.m.** Ulyn texts Elysia: *he's ok. ish. thanks for telling me.* Elysia cries in the bathroom with the fan on.
- **8 p.m.** Kay's mom: *"The school called."* Kay tells her the truth: *"I couldn't get up. I haven't been okay for a while."* Kitchen table. *"You're not a chore I'm managing."* The kettle in the bathroom comes out too. She'll get the heater fixed.
- **Ulyn goes home** for the first time in two weeks. His mom is awake at 10 p.m. They eat leftover khao man gai standing in the kitchen. Neither says much.

### Friday

- Elysia: *tomorrow. 10am. i'm picking u up. no is not an option 😤*

### Saturday

- The natural history museum, in the slime mold room at the back. Elysia has never brought anyone there. Kay tells her why he keeps away from her at school: he thinks *he* makes *her* look weird. Elysia realizes her guess on Day 1 was backwards. She tells him about slime girl, the slime in her bag, and the "version." *"I lied too. I care so much it makes me tired. But I care about you more."*
- Kay: *"You don't have to do the version with me. Ever."* Elysia: *"Then you don't have to be fine with me. Deal?"* They make a pinky promise.
- **After:** The teasing comes back (*"You talk about it like it's your ex." "It's my FRIEND."*), and she says she missed it. They share mango sticky rice. Kay gives her his hoodie, and she keeps it for the rest of the semester. Elysia: *"When you texted couldn't... I know that word. Some mornings I know it."* Kay: *"You don't have to be fine with me either. Both ways."*

## Week 7

- **Monday, the gate:** Kay walks over to Elysia in front of her friends. *"Yes, he's my boyfriend, we all know, moving on."* She holds his hand. Mint gives a tiny thumbs up. Ulyn sees it from the stairs and grins. Ploy, later: *"That was a lot."* Elysia: *"Yeah. I like him a lot."*
- **Monday, after school:** Kay waits in the middle of the gate. *"Walk home with me?"* It's the Day 1 sentence, the other way round. They go past the 7-Eleven and the bus stop. He does an impression of her announcement. *"Your ears were on fire, though." "My ears are on my side. I regret nothing."* She shoves him into a hedge. *"Same time tomorrow?" "Same time tomorrow."*
- **Thursday:** Ulyn walks Kay to the guidance room. Kay knocks. *"I think I've been overwatering."* Ms. Noi: *"And what do you need?"* Afterward Ms. Noi stops Ulyn in the hallway: *"You're the one who walked him. How are you?"* Ulyn: *"Fine!"* She doesn't believe him. *"Thursday's every week."*
- **Friday:** Group meeting in the garden. Ulyn waits and doesn't speak for Kay. Kay: *"I can't do all of it this time."* Bank finds the 2009 photo album, where his uncle is in yellow boots. Fern takes the slides. Ulyn takes the budget. That night there are four cursors in one document.
- Kay feels the soil, finds it damp, and doesn't water.
- Ulyn starts going home for dinner on Fridays.

## Week 8

- Ploy calls Elysia at 1 a.m. Elysia: *"I love you, but I can't do 1 a.m. calls anymore. Lunch tomorrow?"* Ploy sulks for two days, then at lunch: *"You could've said that earlier." "I know."*
- **Tuesday, the garden:** Elysia tells Kay she's scared three years with Ploy are over. Kay tells her about Thursdays: *"I said one thing out loud I'd never said to anyone. Nothing broke."* Then he apologizes properly for the Week 3 slime-in-your-bag joke: *"You said it was fine. I should have known it wasn't. I say that too."* Then: *"You're very bossy. She's probably scared of you." "That was CARE!" "Yes, ma'am."*
- Elysia sits next to Nam in art. Nam draws her a slime mold in the margin of her worksheet.
- Ulyn's dad calls. Ulyn answers for the first time in months. It's awkward, and it's four minutes long.

## Week 9

- **Monday, presentation day:** Bank blanks on the same kind of question as Day 1. Kay **waits** this time. Bank finds it himself: *"Two thousand nine! My uncle was actually in it."* Teacher: *"Good teamwork, everyone."* This time it's true.
- The school keeps the garden, and the agriculture club returns next semester. Bank and Fern have signed up.
- Elysia gives Pun her old notebook from third grade, full of slime mold drawings. *"I was wrong before. Be slime boy. Slime boy is the best."*
- **Friday, dinner at Elysia's:** Pun interrogates Kay (*"What's the best one?" "...The yellow one. Physarum. It's your sister's favorite."*), then reveals she held a funeral for a dried-out slime mold when she was eight, and sang. Kay: *"Was it a nice funeral?"* Elysia, behind her hands: *"...It was a very nice funeral."* At the door her mom calls her *"my easy one."* Kay: *"She's not the easy one. She just makes it look easy."* Elysia wonders why he says it like he knows. Later, in the kitchen, she tells her mom it's true. *"Then you can ask for things. Especially small things."*

## Week 10

### Friday, the last day

- **Kay:** Hot shower. A full fridge and two notes from Mom. At the gate, Elysia shows her friends a photo of a huge yellow slime mold (Pun found it), and she doesn't check their faces. *"Isn't he perfect?" "He's perfect." "Pun named him Kay." "...Should I be flattered?"* She sticks her tongue out at him in front of everyone, wearing his hoodie.
- **Ulyn:** *"Morning. You look— actually, you look okay."* Kay: *"How are you? Actually."* Ulyn: *"...My mom wants me to come home more. I'm trying."* Kay offers to come over. *"Yeah. I'd like that."*
- **Mint** tells Elysia she talked to Kay in Week 2. Elysia: *"You did WHAT."*
- Kay waters the garden once, just enough.
- **Saturday after:** Kay comes over to Ulyn's house. Ulyn's mom makes khao man gai for three. Kay and Ulyn repot her dead balcony plants. *"Too much water or too little?" "Too little. Nobody was home."*
- **Sunday after:** Elysia takes Pun to the slime mold room at the museum, and Kay comes too. She talks the slime girl way the whole time and doesn't check anyone's face.

**Canon endings:** Kay, *Enough*. Ulyn, *Home*. Elysia, *Slime Girl*.
