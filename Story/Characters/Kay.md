# Kay

**Pronouns:** He / they
**Role:** Main character of the first route

## Image

Kay seems calm, reliable, and steady. Classmates come to him for advice and help because he looks like someone who has everything together. He's the group leader by default, the one who knows every date, and the one who finishes everybody's slides at 2:30 in the morning.

Alone with Elysia, he's someone else: he teases her. He goes after her slime-nerd side, her ears going red, and her bossiness ("yes, ma'am"). It's the most himself he ever is. As things get heavier, the teasing is the first thing to go, and Elysia notices before anyone.

Inside, he is constantly overthinking, and that overthinking slowly turns into depressive thoughts. He keeps them hidden so he won't become a burden to anyone.

## Core Struggle

Kay suppresses his own feelings and avoids sharing them. When he was twelve, his dad left. His mom cried at the kitchen table for weeks, and Kay decided he would be the easy one, the kid who never needs anything. Last year he finally reached out to his best friend, Ulyn, late one night. The reply took four days. To Kay, that proved the rule.

So he puts being useful ahead of being honest. He says "it's fine" about cold showers, about classmates who never do their part, and about himself. He gives everything around him more than it needs: his group, his mom, the plants in his front yard. Eventually, he does the same to himself.

## How He Sees the World

Kay sees every situation as a list of ways he could make things worse for someone, and he picks whatever costs other people the least, even when it costs him the most. He assumes he is in the way. He notices other people's tiredness instantly and never notices his own.

## Quiet Desire

He wants to matter without being a burden, and to be accepted without having to explain himself.

## Comfort

**Elysia**, his girlfriend. They rarely talk at school, mostly because Kay keeps his distance, but knowing she's there makes him feel safe and grounded.

**Ulyn**, his best friend. They used to share everything. They've drifted since last year, but they're still friends, and Ulyn is still the person Kay sits next to every day.
