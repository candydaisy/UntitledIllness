# Ulyn

**Pronouns:** He / him
**Role:** Kay's best friend

## Image

Ulyn is loud in a comfortable way. He's the one who makes the class laugh in the middle of a presentation, who knows everyone's name, and who is somehow on the student council, in two clubs, and still writing his chemistry report the night before it's due. People see him as easygoing, blunt, and impossible to embarrass.

He teases Kay constantly. He also notices more about Kay than anyone else does.

## Core Struggle

Ulyn copes by staying busy. Last year his parents separated, and home became a place he avoided. Committees, clubs, extra classes, and other people's problems fill his time so he never has to sit still with his own. Most nights he sleeps at his cousin's place. When his mom calls during class, he declines it.

During the worst week of the separation, Kay messaged him at three in the morning: *"can I call you."* Ulyn saw it and had nothing left to give, so he didn't answer. Four days later he wrote back *"sorry, was busy."* Kay never reached out like that again.

Ulyn has regretted it ever since and has never said so. He watches Kay carry everything alone and gets frustrated. Some of that frustration is with Kay. Most of it is with himself.

## How He Sees the World

Ulyn trusts what people do more than what they say. "I'm fine" means nothing to him, but going quiet, sleeping late, or offering to do someone else's work tells him everything. He reads a room quickly and uses jokes to steer it.

He believes honesty is easy for everyone except himself. He can tell Kay to stop carrying everything while carrying a whole house's worth of silence of his own.

## Quiet Desire

He wants a second chance to be there for Kay the way he wasn't last year.

Underneath that, he wants someone to ask how *he* is doing and wait for the real answer.

## Comfort

Ordinary time with Kay: sitting next to him in class, arguing about nothing over fried rice. Kay is the steadiest part of Ulyn's day, even while they're distant. Noise helps too. Loud places mean he doesn't have to think.

## Relationship to Kay

They became friends on the first day of high school, two years ago, and for most of their first year they told each other everything. Since the unanswered message they still sit together, joke, and walk to class, but neither of them says anything real. Kay thinks Ulyn got tired of him. Ulyn thinks Kay gave up on him. Both are waiting for the other to try first.
