# Elysia — The Version

*The same semester, from the one who is always checking.*

Elysia can't see inside Kay's head, or Ulyn's, or anyone's. She reads faces for a living, and she still guesses wrong.

## How to read this

The format is the same as `Story/Kay/Kay_Story.md`:
- Narration is present tense. Elysia's thoughts are in *italics*. Text messages are `in code`.
- **◆ Choice** marks a decision, with its type, how it changes her Mask and Energy, and any flag it sets. **⏱** means the choice is timed. **★** marks the **canon** choice (see `Story/Canon_Timeline.md`).
- **▸** marks what happens after each option. **◇** marks where the branches meet again. **[If …]** marks a passage that depends on a flag or on her meters.
- **Check:** lines are Elysia scanning faces before she lets herself react. They're part of the "version." As her Mask drops, they appear less often. Visually this could become a small UI element later.

### How Elysia's route plays

Elysia has two hidden meters instead of Kay's Emotional Weight. The player never sees the numbers.

- **Mask** is how much she's performing "the version". Performing raises it, and being real lowers it.
- **Energy** is what it's costing her. Performing drains it. Being real usually restores it, but not always.
- **Choice types:** `perform` (the version), `honest`, `helpful`, `neutral`.
- **⏱ Timed choices** happen when people are waiting on her. She gets 8 seconds at Mask 0 and 4 seconds at Mask 100. When time runs out, she says the `perform` answer.
- **Locks:** honest choices lock when Energy is below 20 or Mask is above 85.
- **✎ Self-edit:** at Mask 60 or higher, a marked line is typed out, deleted, and replaced with the version. Below 60 she just says the real line. The log shows the deleted line crossed out.

**Start:** Mask 60, Energy 60. **Endings:** Mask above 69 → *The Version* · Mask below 50 and Energy above 54 → *Slime Girl* · anything else → *Half a Version*.

### People only Elysia's route introduces

- **Ploy**: leader of Elysia's friend group. Loud, generous, dramatic. Calls Elysia whenever anything goes wrong, which is often.
- **Jane** and **Bow**: the rest of the group. They follow Ploy's lead.
- **Mint**: Elysia's best friend since kindergarten. In a different class. Says exactly what she thinks.
- **Pun**: Elysia's little brother, eight years old. Has a jar on his windowsill that is always occupied.
- **Nam**: a quiet girl in Elysia's class. Draws in the margins of everything. Has a plush frog keychain on her bag.
- **Mom**: Elysia's mom. Kind, busy. Thinks Elysia is "the easy one."

## Chapter 2 — Pad Kra Pao

She planned it. She'll admit that to herself, though never to anyone else.

"Pad kra pao again? You're so predictable."

"Y-you're one to talk. You're in the same line."

"Mm. And why do you think I'm in this line?"

She watches him think about it. She can see him get there, *because of you*, and then back away from it, the way he backs away from everything.

"Because it's good?"

*Coward.* She thinks it fondly.

"Wow. Okay. Sure. Because it's good."

She rolls her eyes. She's still smiling. She can't help it around him.

"You know, I always have to come find you. You never come find me."

"Come sit with us if you want, okay?"

## Chapter 3 — Shouldn't

After school, Kay is halfway through the gate.

*Do it. Just do it. You're allowed to walk home with your own boyfriend.*

"Kayyy~ Wanna walk home together?"

She says it loud and bright, so it sounds easy. Behind her, Ploy and Jane are watching. Ploy whispers something to Jane. Elysia doesn't hear what. She hears the whisper.

Kay looks at her, then past her at her friends, and his face does something careful.

"I... shouldn't. You don't want people looking at us like that."

For half a second, something in her face drops. She can feel it go, like missing a step.

Then the version catches her.

"Oh! Okay. That's fine! No worries."

*Smile. Smile. Smile.*

"See you tomorrow, then."

She waves and walks off alone. She keeps the smile on all the way past the 7-Eleven, the bus stop, and the corner. It stays on a beat too long, then ten beats, then a hundred.

*"You don't want people looking at us like that."*

*Like what?*

*Like the slime girl and the boy who has to stand next to her.*

She knows it's not fair. Kay has never said anything like that. Kay is the gentlest person she knows.

*But he did come to the table, and then the second my friends were watching, he said no.*

*He's embarrassed of me.*

*Like everyone. Like third grade.*

## Chapter 5 — The Slime Mold

The second week, Ploy plans a mall trip for Wednesday.

"Elysia, you're coming, right?"

"I can't, I have to help my brother with a thing."

It's a lie. She's never lied to Ploy about plans before. She doesn't even know why she's doing it, except that she knows Kay has been at the school garden every afternoon. She's seen him from the art room window, alone, holding the hose.

---

The garden behind the science building has four beds. Kay's group has cleared all four. The seedlings are tiny, and the soil is very, very wet.

Kay is holding a hose. He looks up and sees her, and for a second his face does something unguarded. *Surprised. Happy?*

Then he closes it again, politely. "Hi."

"Hi! I was in the area."

"You were in the area. Behind the science building."

"I'm *very* outdoorsy."

She crouches by the chili bed to look at the seedlings, mostly so he can't see her face.

And then she sees it.

On the old log edging the bed, where the soil is wettest, something bright yellow has spread across the bark. It looks like spilled egg yolk, branching into veins like a tiny map.

The version goes completely offline.

"Oh. *Oh.* Kay. Come here. Don't move fast."

He crouches next to her.

"That's a slime mold. *Physarum*, I think. It's not a mold, it's not a plant, it's not an animal, it doesn't even have a brain, and it can still solve a maze. Scientists put oat flakes down in the shape of the cities around Tokyo, and it grew a network that looked almost exactly like the real train lines—"

She hears herself. Loud. Fast. *Weird.*

She stops in the middle of the sentence and glances over her shoulder at the path.

*Check: nobody. Nobody's there. Check again.*

She laughs, too bright. "Sorry. That's weird. Nobody cares about slime."

## Chapter 10 — Couldn't

The sixth Thursday, Kay isn't at the gate.

She always knows when he's there, and this morning she knows he isn't.

`K? didn't see u at the gate 👀` (8:15)

Delivered. Not read.

Not read.

`i couldn't get up today`

She reads it seven times.

*Couldn't.* Not *didn't*. Not *slept in*. Not *sick, lol*.

*Couldn't.*

She knows that word. She has never said it out loud, but she has felt it on some mornings: lying in bed, listening to Ploy's voice in her head and Mom's *easy one* and her own *smile, smile, smile*, and her body simply refusing.
